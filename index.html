Shortcuts & tips
  • Space play/pause · ←/→ 5 s · ,/. one frame · A/B set loop · L clear loop
  • M mirror practice · N mirror official · [/] nudge sync one frame · F fullscreen
  • Zoom into a member: scroll or pinch on a video, drag to move, double-click to reset.
  • If your recording has several runs that restart the song, go to a later run and press Re-sync from here.
  • If the auto-sync is off, press Manual sync, line up a moment (e.g. a clap or the first beat) on both videos, then Lock sync.
  • Follow a member: press Find dancers, then tap the member in your practice video and the same member in the official video. If the close-up ever follows the wrong person, pause and tap the right one.
  • Mirrors: press Ignore area and drag a box over the mirror so reflections aren't treated as dancers. People whose feet are inside the box are ignored.
  • Formation overlay: shows the official video see-through over yours, lined up by the formation's center and size. O toggles it, C toggles close-up, D toggles dancer boxes.
